# Ranna: Your Culinary Community

Build a Modern Recipe Sharing & Food Community Web App — “Ranna”

Create a beautiful, modern, responsive recipe-sharing community platform called “Ranna”.

Ranna is a social recipe platform where people can discover recipes, publish their own recipes, save recipes, like recipes, comment on recipes, and follow food creators.

The product should feel like a combination of Pinterest + modern recipe website + social community, but with its own unique visual identity.

The primary audience is food lovers and home cooks, with a strong focus on Bangladeshi and South Asian cuisine, while still supporting international recipes.

12. COOKING INSTRUCTIONS

Create numbered cooking steps.

01 — Marinate the meat

Mix the mutton with yogurt, spices, ginger and garlic. Allow it to marinate for at least 2 hours.

02 — Prepare the rice

Wash and partially cook the basmati rice with aromatic spices.

03 — Layer everything

Place marinated meat at the bottom and layer potatoes and rice on top.

04 — Add saffron

Add saffron-infused milk and fried onions.

05 — Dum cook

Cover tightly and cook on low heat until the meat becomes tender and the rice is fully cooked.

Each step can optionally have an image.
